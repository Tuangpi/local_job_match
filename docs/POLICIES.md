# Operational Policies and Legal Readiness

## Cancellation
- Customers may cancel an open job before quotation acceptance.
- After booking, cancellation should be recorded through booking status transitions and audit logs.
- Reopening a cancelled or disputed booking requires an admin.

## Disputes and Complaints
- Customers and experts can file reports against a booking.
- Admins review and resolve reports; resolutions are audited.
- Safety or hazardous situations follow the escalation process in SECURITY.md.

## Reviews
- Only the booking customer may review.
- One review per booking.
- Reviews are allowed only after completion.

## Privacy
- Public profiles do not expose phone numbers, addresses, or identity documents.
- Private job and booking details are visible only to participants and admins.
- Uploaded files are stored privately and served only after authorization.

## Expert Verification
- Verification decisions are made by admins and recorded in audit logs.
- Unverified experts are clearly labeled.
- Verification status is included in public profiles.

## Payments
- The pilot does not process payments through the platform.
- Customers and experts agree on direct payment.
- The platform records agreed quotation amounts without representing payment as verified.

This document is not legal advice; obtain local review before launch.
